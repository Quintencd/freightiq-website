RFID stock tracking questions

What is RFID stock tracking software?It is software that links RFID tag reads to inventory records, warehouse movements, receiving, stocktakes, transfers and dispatch proof.
Can RFID replace barcode scanning?RFID can speed up bulk reading and movement proof, while barcodes and QR codes are still useful for document context and item identification.
Can a phone read RFID inventory tags?A phone can scan FlowIQ document QR codes, but UHF RFID stock tags normally require RFID-capable readers or encoders.
Does RFID automatically change stock?FlowIQ keeps RFID evidence review-gated so noisy reads do not automatically change stock, FIFO, invoices or accounting records.
