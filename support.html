FlowIQ Operations for Shopify

We can help with installation, OAuth permissions, reconnecting, account linking, Shopify-billed plans, guided migration, warehouse mapping, stock and price synchronization, and order flow.

  • Connection issue: include the permanent .myshopify.com store domain and the time of the error.
  • Import issue: include the import-run status and the affected SKU, order, or location.
  • Billing issue: state whether the subscription started in FlowIQ or through Shopify. Never email passwords or access tokens.

Privacy and data requests

For Shopify customer-data access, correction, export, or deletion assistance, email support and identify the merchant store. FlowIQ verifies the request before releasing or redacting data.
