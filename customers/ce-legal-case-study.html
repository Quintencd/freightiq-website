The Professional Services Problem

Before FlowIQ, C&E Legal was managing the business through fragmented systems: separate subscriptions for different workflows, staff management and operational follow-up. The systems did not talk to each other, which made it harder to see work allocation, client progress, accounting tasks, payroll activity, invoicing, cash-flow follow-ups and business bottlenecks from one place.

Before FlowIQ

  • Disconnected business systems across different workflows.
  • Staff tasks, follow-ups, invoicing, accounting and payroll visibility were fragmented.
  • The founder needed more manual check-ins to understand client progress and bottlenecks.
  • Client financial work and internal business operations were harder to view together.

After FlowIQ

  • One FlowIQ ecosystem across SalesIQ, WorkIQ, InvoiceIQ, TaskIQ, AccountingIQ and PayrollIQ.
  • AccountingIQ HQ manages 18 client financial workflows from one oversight layer.
  • Better visibility across staff work, client progress, bottlenecks and cash-flow follow-ups.
  • Improved staff capacity to take on more clients as the business scales.
