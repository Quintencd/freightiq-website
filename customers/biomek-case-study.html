The Operational Problem

Before FlowIQ, Biomek Pty Ltd ran across three different systems plus spreadsheets. Imports, landed cost calculations, supplier ordering, sales forecasts, SKU forecasts, sales history, stock projections, stock takes, stock transfers and warehouse-level landed cost recalculations were fragmented. Tasks were managed manually through email, and disconnected workflows forced staff to cross-check formulas, spreadsheets, supplier orders, warehouse movement and reporting manually. The business had one full-time bookkeeping/accounting role and one part-time person supporting paperwork-heavy processes, while also looking at two extra warehouse admin hires to keep up with operational load.

Before FlowIQ

  • Imports, landed cost, stock takes and stock transfers managed through spreadsheets.
  • Three separate systems plus spreadsheets created duplicated work and double entry.
  • Month-end and bookkeeping work required heavy manual checking.
  • Warehouse-level stock decisions were harder to make quickly.
  • Leadership spent more time cross-checking reports and staff inputs.

After FlowIQ

  • One operating view across imports, inventory, forecasting, payroll and accounting.
  • R60,000-R70,000/month operational capacity and system-cost savings reported.
  • Better landed cost visibility, cash-flow planning and stock allocation.
  • Founder time shifted toward business growth instead of manual system management.
