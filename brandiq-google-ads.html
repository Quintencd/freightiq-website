What the connection does

Google Ads shows delivery. BrandIQ connects it to the business result.

BrandIQ uses the Google Ads account you select to report performance and prepare actions. Supported changes remain subject to the campaign, account, budget and safety approvals configured by your business.

The connection flow

No passwords, API keys or manual database setup.

Your administrator signs in on Google's secure page. FlowIQ stores the resulting OAuth credential encrypted, isolates the selected account to your business, and removes the stored credential when you disconnect.

  1. 01
    Choose Connect Google AdsBrandIQ explains the requested access before OAuth begins.
  2. 02
    Sign in with GoogleGoogle authenticates you and displays the requested permissions.
  3. 03
    Select your advertising accountBrandIQ uses only the eligible account your business chooses.
  4. 04
    Measure and approveReporting, recommendations and supported actions remain visible and controlled.
Your control remains in place

Connecting Google Ads does not authorise bank payments, wallet top-ups or unrestricted automated spending. FlowIQ does not sell Google Ads data, combine identifiable advertising data between customers, or use Google API data to train a general-purpose AI model. Availability of particular Google Ads functions depends on Google approval and the permissions held by the connected user.
