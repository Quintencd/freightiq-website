Clear answers

How the safe intake workflow works.

What does auto-split mean?

Each supported email attachment becomes its own independently reviewable queue item. A three-attachment email creates three items; FlowIQ does not silently split a multi-document PDF into guessed page ranges.

Does an incoming email create an order automatically?

No. Email receipt creates a source-linked review item. A user must check fields, map customer or supplier and products, then explicitly create the normal pro-forma or draft purchase order.

How are customer and supplier documents kept apart?

Use separate recipient aliases, or separate Google mailbox labels. FlowIQ follows the configured route and does not guess whether a document belongs in InvoiceIQ or PurchaseIQ.

How does FlowIQ keep the process safe?

Items are organization-scoped and deduplicated, source evidence remains available during review, uncertain mappings stay visible, and no commercial posting occurs from intake alone.

Availability and trust boundary

Incoming email monitoring uses a connected Google mailbox with read-only access. Wider availability depends on Google restricted-scope approval. Auto Capture remains review-first while real-document certification evidence continues to grow.
