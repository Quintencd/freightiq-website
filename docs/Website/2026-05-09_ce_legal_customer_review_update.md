## Copy Guardrails

- The platform-comparison language is usable because it is client-authored review copy.
- The full-system replacement language is usable because the client confirmed they replaced their disconnected systems with FlowIQ.
- Do not use the customer's stronger lines in a way that sounds like FlowIQ guarantees business outcomes for every customer.
- Keep the already confirmed proof points visible: disconnected systems replaced, six staff, FlowIQ Growth Plan and live FlowIQ usage.

## Regression Risks

- 10-15%: Stronger comparative wording can read as too broad if it is not clearly attributed to the customer review. Mitigation: the page labels the comparison and replacement statements as client-authored or client-confirmed review claims.
- 10-15%: The C&E page now emphasizes the founder review more than the previous AccountingIQ HQ operational detail. Mitigation: AccountingIQ, payroll, tasks, cash flow and the system-replacement proof remain visible in the metrics and body copy.
- 10-15%: Additional review sections make the page longer. Mitigation: content is split into scannable panels and quotes, preserving the existing case-study structure and mobile grid behavior.
